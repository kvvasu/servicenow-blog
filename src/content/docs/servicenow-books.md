## ServiceNow Books
No single book covers the whole platform, and release notes age quickly. Pick titles for the role you hold now: administration, development, or CMDB and Discovery work. Read the chapters on the data model and access control closely, because those decisions outlive any script. Check every claim against current product documentation before you trust it.
