Platform projects slip on environment availability and sequencing more often than on effort estimates. This checklist looks at those dependencies first.

## Checklist for Project Plan Reviews
### Clarity
A project plan should break work into chunks a team can estimate and track. Too coarse, and nobody notices schedule slip until the deadline. Too fine, and the plan becomes a task log that maintenance consumes. The reviewer checks that each phase has a deliverable, an owner, and a date, and that the level of detail stays consistent. Uneven decomposition usually hides the riskiest work.
### Completeness
Plans fail when they hide their assumptions. A schedule that assumes the integration team is free in March collapses the moment that guess proves wrong. The same happens when the plan assumes a vendor delivers the API specification on time. The reviewer confirms every assumption is written down, that someone validated it, and that the plan names a fallback. An unstated assumption is a risk nobody owns.
### Consistency
Related parts of a plan must tell the same story. If the scope section names four environments while the schedule funds two, the budget and the timeline disagree with the goal. Dates, resource names, and effort estimates should match everywhere they appear, appendices and charts included. Reviewers should trace each figure back to its source, because small inconsistencies erode trust in the whole document.
