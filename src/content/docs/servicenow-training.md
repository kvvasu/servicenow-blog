## ServiceNow Certifications
ServiceNow splits certifications by role. Certified System Administrator covers platform fundamentals, Certified Application Developer covers scripting, and each product family has its own implementation exams. Those exams require paid vouchers and, on most tracks, a current mainline certification as a prerequisite. Holders keep credentials valid by passing delta exams when a new family release ships. Sales and pre-sales accreditations stay partner-only. Confirm prerequisites on the official certification page before booking.
