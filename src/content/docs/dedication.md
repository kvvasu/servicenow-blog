A short page acknowledging the practitioners, trainers and writers whose published work this site is built around.

## Dedication
A dedication page names the mentor who taught a skill that no certification covers. In ServiceNow work that often means someone who explained why an ACL denies a request, or why a business rule order changed a result. Naming that person records where the knowledge came from. Good platform engineers pass the same teaching forward, because the platform changes and the explanations must keep up.
