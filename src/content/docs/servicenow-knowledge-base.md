## Browse ServiceNow Knowledge Base Support and Troubleshooting Articles
ServiceNow publishes a support knowledge base that answers most platform questions directly. Articles cover known errors, patch behaviour, and the exact steps a support engineer would take. Search by the error string rather than the feature name, because the article title usually quotes the message. Check the published and updated dates, since older entries describe behaviour from a release you no longer run. Reading the article before opening a case saves days and often names the fix.
