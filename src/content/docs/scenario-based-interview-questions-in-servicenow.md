## Scenario based interview questions in ServiceNow
Scenario questions test judgement, not recall. An interviewer describes an incident that keeps reopening and asks how you would find the cause. A strong answer names the tooling: the transaction log, business rule order, and the ACL that hides a field from a user. Weak candidates guess at a fix. Prepare by walking through failures you have handled, and explain why the first attempt failed. Interviewers respect a clear account of a wrong turn more than a rehearsed success story.
### From “ServiceNow World”
This collection gathers scenario interview questions from a ServiceNow World series. Each episode walks a realistic problem, often around Script Includes, then reasons toward an answer instead of reciting syntax. A candidate who studies only syntax fails these; the interviewer wants the reasoning path and the tradeoffs. Watch the Script Include episode first if time is short, because that concept appears inside almost every other scenario.

### From “Learn ServiceNow with Ravi Gaurav”
Ravi Gaurav's series presents real-time scenario questions in episodes, opening with a broad first day. The format matters: the host states a problem an administrator would actually meet, then works through the diagnosis. Treat each episode as a drill. Pause before the answer and write the query or script first. The gap between your attempt and the host's answer teaches more than passive watching.
